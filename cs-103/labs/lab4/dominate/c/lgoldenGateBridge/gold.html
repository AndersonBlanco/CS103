Structure

  • Location: Connects San Francisco to Marin County.
  • Length: Approximately 1.7 miles (2.7 km).
  • Height: Towers rise 746 feet (227 m) above water.
  • Design: Suspension bridge with Art Deco elements.
  • Color: International Orange for visibility and aesthetics.

Visitors and Landmark

  • Walkways: Accessible for pedestrians and cyclists.
  • Scenic Views: Panoramic views of San Francisco Bay and city skyline.
  • Traffic: Carries vehicular traffic across the bay.
  • Maintenance: Regular upkeep to prevent corrosion and structural issues.
  • Lighting: Iconic lighting enhances visibility at night.
