Geography and Structure

  • Location: San Francisco Bay, 1.25 miles (2 km) offshore from San Francisco.
  • Size: Approximately 22 acres (8.9 hectares).
  • Elevation: Highest point around 135 feet (41 meters).
  • Accessibility: Only reachable by ferry.
  • Surrounding Waters: Strong currents and tides typical of the Bay.

History and Visitors

  • Former Use: Federal prison from 1934 to 1963.
  • Notable Inmates: Al Capone, George “Machine Gun” Kelly.
  • Current Use: National Recreation Area with guided tours.
  • Visitor Facilities: Walking tours, ferry access, audio guides.
  • Historic Structures: Cellhouse, lighthouse, guard towers, warden’s house.
